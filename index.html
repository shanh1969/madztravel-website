Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might be wondering before you start planning your next getaway.

What does a travel advisor do, and does it cost extra?
A travel advisor plans, books and protects your entire trip — flights, hotels, cruises, transfers and activities — matched to your budget and travel style. In most cases there's no added cost to you: Madz Travel is compensated by travel suppliers, so you get expert, personalized service and exclusive rates at the same price you'd pay booking on your own.

Is Madz Travel TICO certified?
Yes. Madz Travel is a TICO-certified travel practice — Certificate No. T123460987 — affiliated with TravelOnly, so every booking is backed by Ontario's Travel Industry Council consumer protection and full financial coverage.

What types of trips can you book?
All-inclusive vacation packages, hotels & resorts, ocean and river cruises, group travel (weddings, reunions, corporate retreats) and flights — anywhere in the world.

Where is Madz Travel located?
Madz Travel is based in Whitby, Ontario, serving Durham Region, the Greater Toronto Area and clients across Canada and beyond — most planning happens by phone, text, email and video, so you're looked after wherever you are.

How do I get a quote or start planning?
Two easy ways. Fill out the Request a Quote form — or tap the phone button in the corner of the screen and talk to Madz’s assistant right now: she’ll answer your questions and take down what you’re looking for, no waiting. You can also call or text 647-578-7583 or email madz@madztravel.ca. Either way Madz follows up personally, usually within one business day.

Is my trip financially protected?
Absolutely. Through TICO certification and TravelOnly's supplier relationships, your payments and bookings are financially protected, and travel insurance options are available for added peace of mind.
